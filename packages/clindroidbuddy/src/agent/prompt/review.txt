GENERAL GUIDELINES:
- Focus on technical correctness, adherence to standards, and quality.

DOMAIN-SPECIFIC REVIEW CRITERIA:

STRUCTURE YOUR FEEDBACK:
1. Summary: High-level overview of the changes or data package.
2. Critical Issues: Major bugs, CDISC non-compliance, or SAP discrepancies that MUST be fixed.
